# The Janus bust: licence

`apps/web-console/public/models/janus-wire.glb` (built by `../wire.py`) and its stills
`janus-attack.webp` and `janus-defense.webp` (captured by `../stills.cjs`) are made from
**"Bust of Two-Faced Janus"**, a 3D scan of the bust in the State Russian Museum, St Petersburg,
published by **Scan The World** on MyMiniFactory:
<https://www.myminifactory.com/object/3d-print-bust-of-two-faced-janus-30038>

Changes made: centred and scaled, reduced from 700 000 to 5 200 flat triangles
(`assets/janus/wire.py`), drawn in the console as a wireframe; two stills of that wireframe, one
per face (`assets/janus/stills.cjs`). The raw STL is not in the repository: download it from the link above
and place it in this folder to rebuild.
