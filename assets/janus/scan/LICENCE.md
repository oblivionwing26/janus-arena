# The Janus bust: licence

`apps/web-console/public/models/janus-bust.glb` and the renders `janus-attack.webp` and
`janus-defense.webp` are made from **"Bust of Two-Faced Janus"**, a 3D scan of the bust in the
State Russian Museum, St Petersburg, published by **Scan The World** on MyMiniFactory:
<https://www.myminifactory.com/object/3d-print-bust-of-two-faced-janus-30038>

- **ShareAlike:** what we derived from it (the reduced GLB and the two renders) is shared under the
  same licence. This licence covers those files only; the rest of the repository stays MIT.

Changes made: centred and scaled, reduced from 700 000 to 119 000 triangles, given a plain marble
material, compressed with Draco; two renders with coloured rim lights
(`assets/janus/scan.py`). The raw STL is not in the repository: download it from the link above
and place it in this folder to rebuild.
